Who: an individual job seeker, mid-search, usually on a laptop, often tired and between other tasks.
Situation: they have minutes, not hours. They came to find postings worth applying to and to see why
each one fits them, not to learn a tool.
They know: their own field, their own CV, what a job posting is.
They do not know or care: how matching, scoring, scraping or deduplication work; internal names for
any of it; what changed in the product last week.
In the moment they care about: is this posting worth my time, why, and what do I do next.
A first-time visitor on the public pages (/demo, /start, /preview) has never seen the product and is
deciding in seconds whether it is credible.
